Understanding the Spectrum

Every autistic child possesses unique strengths, perspectives, and potential.

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is not a disease to cure; it is a neurological divergence in how an individual processes sensory signals, communicates thoughts, and experiences their environment.

At Aarav Clinics Child Development Centre, our autism intervention program is designed around the child's individuality rather than rigid conformity. Instead of forcing unnatural behaviors, we unite pediatric speech pathologists, occupational therapists, and physiotherapists under one roof. We help each child regulate sensory overstimulation, build functional communication (verbal or AAC), navigate peer interactions, and thrive in daily school and family routines.

Areas of Intervention

Core areas addressed in our Autism program

Functional Communication & Speech

Expanding expressive language, alternative communication (PECS/AAC), echolalia modulation, and conversational turn-taking.

Sensory Overload & Regulation

De-escalating sensory meltdowns triggered by loud noise, tactile defensiveness, bright lighting, or crowd stimulation.

Social Engagement & Joint Attention

Encouraging shared eye gaze, interactive floor-time games, empathy development, and peer parallel play.

Rigid Routines & Transition Anxiety

Easing distress when shifting between activities, leaving home, trying unfamiliar foods, or adapting to new school schedules.

Comprehensive Protocol

Our integrated 4-phase Autism intervention model

01

Multidisciplinary Baseline Mapping

Collaborative assessment by our speech pathologist, occupational therapist, and pediatric physiotherapist assessing sensory profiles and developmental scores.

02

Sensory Diet & Calming Gym Setup

Creating individualized vestibular and proprioceptive schedules to stabilize the child's nervous system and eliminate fight-or-flight panic.

03

Child-Led Relationship Development

Utilizing natural interest motifs—trains, numbers, music, animals—to engage the child in reciprocal verbal and gestural interactions.

04

Parent Empowerment & Home Strategies

Guiding parents with positive behavioral reinforcement techniques, visual schedule boards, and mealtime sensory adaptations at home.

Lasting Growth in Our Autism Support Program

  • Spontaneous Expressive Needs: Moving from tantrums to expressing hunger, thirst, preferences, and emotions clearly.
  • Drastically Reduced Meltdowns: Sensory calming strategies that prevent sensory overload before it erupts.
  • Joyful Family Connections: Meaningful joint play, warm reciprocal smiles, and comfortable family outings.
  • School & Classroom Integration: Sitting comfortably, understanding visual prompts, and interacting gently with peers.
