Clinical Excellence at Home

Recovery should never be compromised by the difficulty of travelling.

Navigating city traffic, climbing staircases, and enduring long car rides immediately after joint replacement surgery or following a neurological event can aggravate acute pain and increase injury risk.

Aarav Clinics provides comprehensive Home Physiotherapy services across Hyderabad. Our licensed physiotherapists travel directly to your doorstep equipped with portable electrotherapy units, ultrasound modalities, resistance training bands, balance cushions, and measurement tools. We recreate the clinical rehabilitation standard right in the familiar comfort of your home.

Who Benefits Most

Ideal candidates for In-Home Physiotherapy

Post-Surgical Joint Replacement

Immediate phase recovery following Total Knee Replacement (TKR), Total Hip Replacement (THR), or spinal fusion surgery.

Acute Stroke & Neurological Recovery

Bedbound hemiparetic patients, transfer retraining, spasticity management, and home obstacle navigation.

Geriatric Frailty & Fall Risk

Elderly family members with general muscle deconditioning, osteoporosis, Parkinson's disease, or fear of falling.

Severe Acute Pain & Disc Lock

Acute lumbar disc bulges with incapacitating spasms preventing standing or sitting in a car.

Service Process

How our in-home therapy visits work

01

Initial Case Review & Dispatch

We review surgical notes or clinical history over phone/WhatsApp to assign the ideal specialized physiotherapist and equip them appropriately.

02

Comprehensive Bedside Assessment

Evaluating joint range, swelling, vital signs, muscle strength, bed mobility, and home environment fall risks.

03

Hands-On Therapeutic Session

Delivering pain-relieving electrotherapy, manual mobilization, gentle passive stretching, and safe standing or assisted walking drills.

04

Family Caregiver Guidance

Training family members and attendants on safe transfer mechanics, position changes, and daily exercise compliance between visits.

Key Advantages of In-Home Physiotherapy

  • Zero Travel Stress: Eliminate uncomfortable car rides, bumpy transit, and parking hassles entirely.
  • Real-World Environment Training: Practicing bed transfers, couch seating, and bathroom navigation in the patient's actual home.
  • Uninterrupted Recovery Continuity: Beginning rehabilitation immediately from day 1 post-hospital discharge.
  • Dedicated 1-on-1 Focus: A full 45 to 60 minutes of completely uninterrupted clinical attention from your personal therapist.
