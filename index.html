Patient FAQ

Questions, answered clearly.

We believe in complete clinical transparency. If you have any additional questions about therapy protocols or scheduling, please reach out to our desk.

Contact Clinical Desk →

Your initial 45-minute visit includes a comprehensive clinical discussion of your medical history, symptoms, daily posture, and previous scans. Your specialist conducts hands-on biomechanical tests, pinpoints root causes, and details your personalized recovery milestones.

A doctor's referral is not strictly required. You can consult our physiotherapists and child specialists directly. However, if you already have physician prescriptions, post-operative discharge summaries, or MRI/X-ray reports, please bring them so we can review them in detail.

Our child development department coordinates pediatric physiotherapists, speech therapists, and sensory occupational therapists together. Rather than fragmented visits, your child receives a unified program where milestones are monitored across communication, sensory processing, and motor control.

For patients recovering from major surgery, elderly family members, or those with severe mobility constraints, our licensed physiotherapists travel directly to your residence across Hyderabad with necessary portable therapy equipment.

Many acute conditions experience noticeable symptom relief within 3 to 5 sessions. Complex spinal, neurological, or developmental conditions require consistent, progressive therapy over several weeks. Your therapist provides clear, measurable benchmarks during your first evaluation.
