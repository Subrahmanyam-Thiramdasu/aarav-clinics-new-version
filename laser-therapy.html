Class IV Photobiomodulation
Deep Tissue Cellular Healing
Targeted Cellular Energy

Healing injured tissue at the mitochondrial level.

Class IV Therapeutic Laser Therapy delivers specific wavelengths of red and near-infrared light deep into damaged subcutaneous tissues, penetrating tendons, ligaments, and arthritic joint capsules that standard superficial modalities cannot reach.

When light photons interact with cytochrome c oxidase in your cells' mitochondria, they trigger a surge in adenosine triphosphate (ATP) production. This cellular energy accelerates natural tissue repair, enhances local microcirculation, rapidly reduces inflammatory cytokines, and desensitizes pain receptors—allowing you to regain active mobility faster.

Clinical Applications

Conditions responsive to Class IV Laser

Plantar Fasciitis & Heel Spurs

Intense morning heel pain, thick plantar fascia inflammation, and calcaneal spur sensitivity.

Knee Osteoarthritis

Cartilage wear, chronic joint effusion, crepitus, and stiff knees upon standing.

Tendinopathies & Tennis Elbow

Lateral epicondylitis, Achilles tendonitis, supraspinatus tears, and golfer's elbow.

Frozen Shoulder & Bursitis

Severe night shoulder throbbing, adhesive capsulitis, and acute subacromial bursitis.

Treatment Experience

What happens during a laser therapy session

01

Tissue Diagnostic Mapping

Pinpointing the exact anatomical source of pain, depth of target tissue, and chronicity to calibrate dosage parameters.

02

Protective Optical Eyewear

Both patient and clinician wear certified wavelength-filtering protective glasses to ensure complete ocular safety.

03

Dynamic Non-Contact Handpiece Sweep

The calibrated laser wand glides smoothly over the affected joint or tendon, transmitting a soothing, warm therapeutic sensation.

04

Immediate Functional Mobilization

Following laser application when tissue is relaxed and desensitized, our physiotherapist performs gentle joint gliding and active loading.

Why Patients Choose Class IV Laser Therapy

  • Completely Painless: No needles, no electrical shocks, no burning; patients only feel a soothing, comfortable warmth.
  • Zero Downtime: Return to work or gentle daily activities immediately following each clinical session.
  • Accelerated Healing Timeline: Promotes collagen synthesis and rapid neo-vascularization in chronic tendon zones.
  • Drug-Free Pain Relief: Decreases dependency on non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) and pain injections.
Frequently Asked

Common Questions About High-Intensity Laser Therapy

Class IV therapeutic laser produces non-ionizing infrared light. It does NOT emit harmful ionizing radiation like X-rays. It is fully FDA-approved for musculoskeletal rehabilitation and safe when administered by trained clinical physiotherapists.

Many patients note initial pain reduction and easier movement after 1 to 2 sessions. However, lasting cellular tissue remodeling typically requires a course of 5 to 8 sessions scheduled 2 to 3 times per week, integrated with exercise therapy.

For moderate osteoarthritis, partial tendon tears, bursitis, and adhesive capsulitis, laser therapy combined with physiotherapy frequently resolves pain and restores function sufficiently to avoid invasive surgery. For advanced bone-on-bone degeneration, it provides effective palliative pain relief.
