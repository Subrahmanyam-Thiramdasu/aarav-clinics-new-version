Surgeon-Aligned Care
Knee • Hip • Spine • Shoulder
Clinical Philosophy

A successful surgery requires expert post-operative rehabilitation.

Surgery repairs the structural tissue, but only targeted physical therapy restores muscle firing, normal joint kinematics, and unassisted walking.

At Aarav Clinics, our physiotherapists coordinate directly with your orthopedic surgeon's discharge instructions. We ensure safe, timed progression that protects your surgical graft or prosthetic implant while avoiding arthrofibrosis (joint stiffness).

Surgeries We Rehabilitate

Structured protocols for major orthopedic procedures

Total Knee Replacement (TKR)

Restoring 0° extension and 120°+ flexion, quadriceps firing, and unassisted walking.

Total Hip Replacement (THR)

Hip precaution adherence, abductor muscle reconditioning, and stable gait restoration.

ACL & Meniscus Arthroscopy

Graft protection, swelling drainage, progressive weight bearing, and hamstring/quad balances.

Spine Surgery (Discectomy / Fusion)

Gentle neural mobilization, core lumbar stabilization, and safe ergonomics for daily living.

Phased Pathway

The 4 stages of post-operative recovery

01

Early Protection (Weeks 1–2)

Gentle passive range of motion, ankle pumps to prevent DVT, cryotherapy, and safe bed transfers.

02

Active Range & Gait (Weeks 3–6)

Weaning off walker or crutches, scar tissue mobilization, and active joint flexion improvement.

03

Muscle Strengthening (Weeks 7–12)

Progressive closed-chain resistance training to rebuild surrounding muscle bulk and endurance.

04

Functional Freedom (Month 3+)

Stair climbing confidence, balance training, outdoor walking, and return to independent routines.

Why Early Post-Surgical Rehab is Vital

  • Prevents permanent contractures and frozen joint stiffness
  • Reduces post-operative swelling and pain
  • Home visit options available for the first critical weeks
  • Faster return to independent driving and stair walking
