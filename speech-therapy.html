SPEECH THERAPY

Helping every voice be heard.

Certified speech-language pathology providing comprehensive therapy for speech delays, articulation issues, stammering, and social communication in Ashok Nagar, Hyderabad.

Book Speech Assessment WhatsApp Us
Speech Pathology
Clarity • Fluency • Expression
Unlocking Communication

Communication is fundamental to human connection and self-esteem.

Whether a toddler is struggling with first words, a school-age child is frustrated by articulation barriers, or an adult is managing stammering or post-stroke aphasia, personalized speech therapy restores fluid communication.

Under the direction of Dr. Karthik Varma (MASLP), our speech pathology department combines standardized acoustic evaluations with patient-centered therapeutic methods. We target oral-motor coordination, vocal clarity, receptive comprehension, sentence formulation, and social pragmatics in a warm, welcoming space.

Conditions Treated

Speech and language challenges we address

Speech Delay in Toddlers

Limited vocabulary, lack of gesture-word pairing, and delay in sentence formation.

Articulation & Phonological Errors

Difficulty pronouncing specific speech sounds (lisping, substitution, omission).

Stammering / Stuttering & Cluttering

Repetitions, prolongations, blocks, and vocal tension affecting natural speaking flow.

Adult Aphasia & Apraxia of Speech

Language recovery following stroke or neurological trauma to rebuild word-finding and comprehension.

Clinical highlights at Aarav Clinics

  • Led by certified speech pathologists (MASLP)
  • Engaging, child-friendly therapy tools
  • Parent coaching for language-rich home environments
  • Secure video therapy options available
