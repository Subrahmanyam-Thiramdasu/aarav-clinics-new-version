Neuroplastic Recovery
Gait • Balance • Motor Retraining
Harnessing Neuroplasticity

The human brain has an extraordinary capacity to rewire and recover.

A stroke disrupts neural signalling between the brain and muscular system. However, through structured, task-specific, high-repetition physiotherapy, undamaged brain regions can form new synaptic pathways to restore motor function.

At Aarav Clinics, our stroke rehabilitation program is led by experienced neuro-physiotherapists who understand that recovery does not stop at hospital discharge. Whether you are in the acute post-stroke window (first 3 to 6 months) or managing chronic impairments years later, our focused protocols help rebuild strength, posture, and self-reliance.

Deficits Addressed

Targeted neurological symptoms we treat

Hemiparesis & Hemiplegia

Unilateral weakness or paralysis affecting the upper limb, trunk, or leg on one side of the body.

Spasticity & Muscle Rigidity

Involuntary muscle tightness and hypertonia that restrict joint mobility and cause painful contractures.

Gait & Balance Impairment

Asymmetrical walking patterns, circumduction gait, foot drop, and heightened fear of falls.

Upper Limb Coordination Loss

Loss of fine motor control, hand grip weakness, and difficulty grasping or manipulating eating utensils.

Our Methodology

Evidence-based neurological rehabilitation protocol

01

Task-Specific Motor Practice

Repetitive, goal-directed actions such as reaching, grasping, sitting-to-standing, and stepping to forge new neurological wiring.

02

Spasticity & Tone Modulation

PNF stretching, manual neuromuscular release, positioning techniques, and therapeutic splint guidance to suppress abnormal synergy patterns.

03

Supported Gait & Balance Training

Parallel bar training, body-weight supported step cycles, obstacle stepping, and sensory perturbation for balance confidence.

04

ADL Independence Coaching

Training family caregivers and patients in bed mobility, safe transfer techniques, and adaptive self-care methods for daily living.

Measurable Outcomes of Neuro-Rehabilitation

  • Improved Mobility: Transition from wheelchair or bedbound status to assisted or independent ambulation.
  • Reduced Spastic Stiffness: Release of involuntary muscle contractions, relieving discomfort and preventing deformities.
  • Enhanced Upper Limb Use: Functional retraining of the affected arm and hand for feeding, grooming, and dressing.
  • Decreased Fall Risk: Restored postural reflexes and core stability to protect against recurrent injuries.
