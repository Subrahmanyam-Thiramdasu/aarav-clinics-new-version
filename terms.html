Terms of Service

Guidelines governing therapy consultations, appointments, patient commitments, and rehabilitation services at Aarav Clinics.

Standard Clinical Care
Evidence-Based • Ethical Practice
Last Updated: October 2026

1. Agreement to Terms

By booking an appointment, submitting an inquiry, or receiving therapy services at Aarav Clinics (Ashok Nagar, Hyderabad), you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. If you are scheduling services on behalf of a minor, you affirm that you are the lawful parent or legal guardian.

2. Medical Disclaimer

Information provided on this website—including health tips, developmental articles, and exercise guides—is for educational purposes only. It is not intended to substitute for individualized medical diagnosis, clinical evaluation, or treatment by a licensed physician or rehabilitation professional.

3. Clinical Consultations & Treatment Plans

All physical therapy, speech therapy, and occupational therapy programs begin with a formal assessment. Treatment outcomes vary depending on patient compliance, health status, and regular attendance. Our therapists establish evidence-based goals but cannot guarantee specific timelines for full recovery.

4. Appointments & Cancellations

Website form submissions transmit an appointment request via WhatsApp or phone. An appointment is only confirmed once clinic staff reviews therapist availability and confirms a time slot. We request at least 4 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling.

5. Home Physiotherapy Services

Home visit therapy is subject to therapist availability within Hyderabad localities. Patients must ensure a clean, safe, well-ventilated space for the therapist to conduct manual therapy and exercises safely.

6. Video Consultation Scope

Video consultations are intended for milestone follow-ups, ergonomics guidance, parent coaching, and exercise reviews where an in-person physical examination is not clinically mandatory. If a patient presents acute red-flag symptoms, our clinicians will advise immediate hospital emergency care.

7. Jurisdiction

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any disputes arising in connection with our services shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Hyderabad, Telangana.
