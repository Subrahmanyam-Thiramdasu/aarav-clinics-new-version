Expert Therapy, No Boundaries

Access senior clinical guidance without geographical limits.

Busy corporate schedules, travel constraints, living outside Hyderabad, or residing overseas should not keep you or your child from receiving gold-standard clinical advice.

Aarav Clinics provides structured tele-rehabilitation and online video consultations. Through live visual assessment, our clinicians evaluate posture, joint mobility, active movement patterns, and ergonomic setups. We also review uploaded MRI or diagnostic imaging reports to deliver clear, unbiased second opinions and actionable exercise regimens.

Ideal Consultation Scenarios

What can be addressed effectively over video

Workstation Ergonomics & Desk Pain

Real-time visual assessment of your chair height, monitor distance, neck angle, and lumbar support.

MRI, CT & X-Ray Second Opinions

In-depth clinical interpretation of imaging reports regarding disc bulges, meniscus tears, or surgery need.

Parent Milestone Screening

Guiding parents on toddler speech development, walking delays, or sensory concerns using pre-recorded video clips.

Exercise Form Correction

Live visual feedback ensuring home rehab routines and strengthening drills are executed with safe biomechanics.
